# Conversational Tailoring Questionnaire

## Background

Imagine an AI embodied agent whose role, personality, appearance, or behavior can be changed through natural-language requests during interaction. We refer to this process as **conversational tailoring**.

Unless otherwise specified, use the following scale:

**0 = Not important at all**  
**5 = Extremely important**

---

## A. Overall Need / Motivation

### Q1. Intended Result
**How important is it that the agent’s changes closely match what you originally intended?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q2. Interaction Effort
**How important is it to complete a tailoring request without repeatedly rephrasing or correcting your request?**

Scale: **0–5**

---

## B. Intention Expression & Interpretation

### Q3. Capability Awareness
**How important is it to know what kinds of changes the AI agent is capable of making before you formulate a request?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q4. Interpretation Awareness
**How important is it to know how the system has interpreted your request?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q5. Clarification Strategy
**If your request is ambiguous, how much would you prefer the system to ask for clarification rather than make its own assumption?**

Scale:

- **0 = Strongly prefer the system to decide automatically**
- **5 = Strongly prefer the system to ask for clarification**

### Q6. Interpretation Confirmation
**How important is it for the system to provide a brief confirmation of how it understood your tailoring request before applying the change?**

Scale: **0–5**

---

## C. Conversational Flow & Context

### Q7. Delay Tolerance
**What is the longest response delay you would consider acceptable during a spoken conversational tailoring interaction?**

Options:

- Less than 1 second
- 1–2 seconds
- 2–3 seconds
- 3–5 seconds
- 5–7 seconds
- More than 7 seconds

### Q8. Context Continuity
**How important is it for the agent to remember your recent tailoring requests or relevant preferences so that you do not need to state them again?**

Scale: **0–5**

---

## D. Request Execution & Mismatch Correction

### Q9. Result Visibility
**After a tailoring request, how important is it to clearly know what properties of the agent have actually changed?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q10. Recovery
**How important is it to easily revise or undo a change that does not match your intention?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q11. Capability Transparency
**If the system cannot fully perform the requested change, how important is it for the system to explicitly tell you about this limitation?**

Scale: **0–5**

### Q12. Partial Execution Transparency
**If the system can only partially match your request, how important is it for it to explain which parts were successfully applied and which parts could not be fulfilled?**

Scale: **0–5**

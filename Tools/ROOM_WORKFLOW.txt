CLOUD CONVERSATION ROOM — first environment pass

Scene: Assets/Project/Scenes/CloudConversationRoom.unity
Unity 2022.3.62f3c1 / URP 14 / PICO Android target.

Layout, in metres:
  Clear interior: X 11.5, Y 6.75, Z 21.3333. User looks along +Z.
  Glazing at X -5.75; floor and roof extend to X -6.35; 8 glass bays / 9 vertical frames.
  One 1.8 x 0.85 x 0.74 table, two matching upholstered oak chairs.
  User seat (0, 0, -0.8); tutor seat (0, 0, 0.8), facing -Z.
  User reference eye height 1.2. Actual physical seated fit needs headset testing.

Workflow:
  Open the scene. Menus are under AIAA4435 > Room.
  Build Cloud Conversation Room regenerates the authored environment assets.
  Existing scene contents are copied to SetupBackups/Room before rebuilding;
  dirty open scenes are separately saved as backup copies under Project/Scenes.
  Rebuild is for regenerating this design, not preserving manual edits to its
  generated meshes/materials. Duplicate assets before making independent variants.
  After rebuilding, run AIAA4435 > Room > Lighting > Prepare Mobile Baked Lighting,
  then Bake Production Lighting. For geometry/light changes in the prepared room,
  use Bake Production Lighting. These menus manage the floor bake surface.
  Capture Room Previews saves six actual Unity camera renders under Captures.

Textures:
  Wood: Poly Haven Oak Veneer 01, 4096 x 4096 diffuse and normal, CC0.
  Attribution and verified checksums: Assets/Project/Textures/Room/PolyHaven/SOURCE.txt.
  Floor: grain mapped across 1.8 m and along 2 m, offsets per plank, six tones.
  Table: 1.8 metre longitudinal repeat; grain along the table's long edge.
  Linen: procedural 1024 square texture represents 12 cm on seat/back upholstery.
  Mipmaps + trilinear filtering + 16x anisotropy; Android ASTC 4x4.
  Walls: warm greige #DED8CC. Ceiling: matte warm charcoal #484A48.
  Plain plaster and ceiling intentionally have no coarse decorative texture.
  Tools/generate_room_textures.py retains the original procedural texture sources;
  the final wood materials use the Poly Haven scans instead of those fallbacks.

Clouds:
  CloudSkyBake.shader evaluates a static volume into a 1024-pixel-per-face cubemap.
  Runtime uses a single sky texture, without a per-eye volume ray march.
  The cloud volume is placed 24 m lower than the initial design.
  This is a distant backdrop; it has no local cloud parallax or fly-through support.
  AIAA4435 > Room > Bake Cloud Sky regenerates the background separately.

Integration:
  RoomReferences exposes userSeat, tutorSeat, tutorEye, tableSurface, tableDecor.
  Optional notebook and pen may be hidden by disabling tableDecor, then rebaking
  to remove their shadows from the tabletop.
  The XRI Starter Assets rig retains tracking and controller interaction.
  Locomotion is disabled for the seated conversation task.
  XR Device Simulator is EditorOnly and is omitted from player builds.
  LLM, speech services, tutor avatar and experimental conditions are not implemented.

Quality acceptance:
  Editor screenshots are visual checks only. Final lens clarity, refresh rate,
  comfort, tracking origin and physical chair alignment require PICO 4 Ultra tests.
  90 Hz is a configuration target, not a measured performance result.

  One 1024 x 1024 directional lightmap set (color + direction, ETC2 RGBA8),
